## Why the previous checks missed this

`tools/test-desktop-ui.mjs` checks the dashboard, project flows, storage isolation, grading, and editor behavior through Chromium with a Tauri IPC bridge. Its contrast assertions compare nine theme-token combinations; they do not inspect the rendered lesson controls, their geometry, or native WebKit rendering. The test does not click through the lesson Explore/Quiz/Practice sequence for visual review. These are useful functional checks, but insufficient evidence for child-facing UI quality.
